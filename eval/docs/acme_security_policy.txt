Acme Information Security Policy

Passwords
Passwords must be at least 14 characters long and changed every 180 days. Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for all remote access and for administrator accounts.

Incident reporting
Suspected security incidents must be reported to the security team within 2 hours of discovery, using the hotline or the security@acme.example mailbox.

Data retention
Customer records are retained for 7 years after the end of the contract and then securely deleted. Backups are encrypted with AES-256 and stored in two separate regions.
