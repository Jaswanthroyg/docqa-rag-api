Acme Router X200 Product Manual

Specifications
The Router X200 supports Wi-Fi 6 with a maximum combined throughput of 3000 Mbps. It has four gigabit Ethernet ports and one 2.5 gigabit WAN port. The device weighs 540 grams and is powered by a 12V adapter drawing at most 24 watts.

Setup
Connect the WAN cable, plug in the power adapter, and wait until the status light turns solid green, which takes about 2 minutes. Open the admin page at 192.168.1.1 and log in with the password printed on the label under the device.

Factory reset
To restore factory settings, hold the reset button for 10 seconds while the device is powered on. All custom settings including Wi-Fi names and passwords will be erased.

Warranty
The Router X200 comes with a 24 month limited warranty covering manufacturing defects. Damage caused by water, power surges, or unauthorised firmware is not covered. To claim, contact support with the serial number and proof of purchase.

Firmware updates
Firmware updates are released quarterly. Enable automatic updates in the admin page under System, then Maintenance.
