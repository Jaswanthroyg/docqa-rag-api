Acme Corp Employee Handbook

Leave policy
Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Unused leave up to 10 days can be carried over to the next year. Sick leave is separate and provides 12 paid days per year; a medical certificate is required for absences longer than 3 consecutive days.

Working hours
Core working hours are 10:00 to 16:00 local time. Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager approval. Overtime must be approved in advance and is compensated with time off in lieu.

Expenses
Business travel must be booked through the Acme travel portal. Meals are reimbursed up to 1,500 rupees per day while travelling. Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense, otherwise they will be rejected.

Onboarding
New hires complete a 90-day onboarding plan. A mentor is assigned during the first week, and a probation review takes place at the end of month 3.
